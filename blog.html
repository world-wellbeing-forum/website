Highlights from Dr Sadhna Dixit

Moments, ideas and milestones from Sadhna’s work in wellbeing, yoga, meditation and naturopathy.

PRACTICE

25 years of yoga

Sadhna learned yoga while living in India and has practised for 25 years. She teaches yoga and meditation regularly and travels back to India to spend time in the Himalayas, deepening her own practice and renewing her teaching.

PHILOSOPHY

“If you can change your mind, you can change anything”

Modern life is full of weapons of mass distraction. Sadhna’s approach is to build the skills to find the off-button, relax and focus, developing a strong, balanced and flexible mind.
