The World Wellbeing Kitchen philosophy

Good food should nourish the whole person.

We bring together the comfort of home cooking, the diversity of global vegetarian food and practical nutrition principles. Recipes celebrate vegetables, pulses, whole grains, soy foods, herbs and spices — with dedicated vegan and dairy-inclusive vegetarian recipes. All recipes are egg-free and nut-free; vegan dishes use no dairy, honey or other animal-derived ingredients.

Our approach favours satisfying protein, fibre-rich ingredients, thoughtful portions and delicious flavour. No ingredient has to be complicated, and healthy food should never feel like a punishment.

The forthcoming cookbook

300 recipes.
One more joyful way to wellbeing.

World Wellbeing Kitchen by Sadhna Dixit is being developed as a richly illustrated cookbook with 15 chapters, practical cooking guidance and appliance-specific methods for the stovetop, microwave, conventional oven and air fryer.

Every recipe will include ingredients, step-by-step instructions, preparation and cooking times, serving guidance and carefully estimated nutrition information. Each recipe will explain stovetop, microwave, conventional oven and air-fryer options where those methods are suitable; unsuitable appliance methods will be clearly identified.
