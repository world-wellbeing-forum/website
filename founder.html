A LIFE DEDICATED TO WELLBEING

Focus. Courage. Peace. Purpose.

Sadhna is passionate about helping people develop focus, courage, peace, inner strength, productivity, health, wellbeing, happiness, purpose and ultimately confidence and success.

An author, public speaker, alternative medicine advocate and prominent figure in the New Age movement, she regularly teaches meditation and yoga. She has practised yoga for 25 years, learning while living in India. She is a qualified naturopath and yoga teacher.

She travels regularly to India, spending time in the Himalayas to deepen her own practice and continue developing her teachings in wellbeing, yoga and meditation. Through her books and videos, she shares her perspective on naturopathy and alternative approaches to wellbeing.

WORKING WITH PEOPLE & ORGANISATIONS

Wellbeing for every stage of life.

Sadhna works with a broad cross-section of people and organisations, delivering wellbeing programmes for businesses, Chambers of Commerce, entrepreneurs, schools and individuals.

Teaching & programmes

Yoga, meditation, wellbeing education and programmes for organisations and communities.

Mentoring & life coaching

Mentoring and coaching sessions focused on goals, strengths, clarity, focus and personal development.

Stress support

One-to-one wellbeing sessions intended to help people manage stress and build habits for longer-term wellbeing. These are not a substitute for clinical mental-health care.

EVENTS & COMMUNITY

Rejuvenate India

Sadhna has delivered and organised wellbeing events and programmes, including Rejuvenate India events—a celebration and promotion of wellbeing with more than 500 participants, centred on the themes “Wellbeing at Work” and “Wellbeing for All”.

HER PHILOSOPHY

“If you can change your mind, you can change anything.”

Modern life is full of stresses. With so many weapons of mass distraction, developing skills to find the off-button, relax and build focus are essential to health, happiness and wellbeing. The aim of yoga and meditation is to develop a strong, balanced and flexible mind.

Sadhna offers programmes for individuals, organisations and businesses to help restore wellbeing, find balance, build focus, happiness and success, and unlock personal potential.

Books & writing

Sadhna has written five books on wellbeing and naturopathy. One title supplied for this profile is A Cure to Cancer by Naturopathy. Book titles and descriptions should be confirmed against the published editions before a full catalogue is added.

Books discussing naturopathy or health approaches are not a substitute for evidence-based medical advice, diagnosis or cancer treatment.

Professional & humanitarian experience

Sadhna has worked in the high-stress environment of international emergency response and development programming, responding to man-made and natural disasters in New Zealand and Asia. Her work also includes wellbeing leadership and business roles.

She is CEO of the World Wellbeing Forum, Director of Spiritual Earth in Ireland and Director of Fernleaf Ltd in New Zealand.
