About the Forum

The World Well-Being Forum is a non-profit alliance advancing well-being through research, education, and real-world programs. We convene scientists, clinicians, educators, public servants, and culture-makers to turn evidence into action.

Mission

Improve quality of life at scale by integrating science and tradition, technology and humanity.

What We Do

  • Publish open, practical toolkits
  • Run cohort-based learning programs
  • Support pilots with public institutions
  • Host the annual World Well-Being Summit

Regional Chapters

United States • India • United Arab Emirates • Germany • Japan

About
