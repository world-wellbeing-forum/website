ABOUT WORLD WELLBEING FORUM

Wellbeing for people.
Purpose for life.

A platform founded by Dr Sadhna Dixit to encourage personal growth, healthier work-life integration and wellbeing across individuals, families, organisations and communities.

OUR VISION

Helping people realise their full potential.

We believe wellbeing is built through the choices, relationships and environments that shape everyday life. It includes the body and mind, but also family, friendship, work, finances, digital habits, purpose and connection.

The Forum shares ideas, learning and programmes that help people develop focus, inner strength, health, happiness, confidence and a sense of purpose.

What we stand for

Whole-person wellbeing

Recognise the connections between physical, mental, emotional, social, spiritual, financial, digital and professional wellbeing.

Learning & practice

Make space for yoga, meditation, reflection, healthy routines and lifelong learning.

Work-life integration

Help individuals and organisations create healthier, more sustainable ways of living and working.
