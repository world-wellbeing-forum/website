OUR FOUNDER

Sadhna Dixit

Founder & Visionary Leader · World Well-Being Forum

A life connecting
knowledge and service.

Sadhna Dixit’s work brings together international business experience, natural well-being, humanitarian service, environmental awareness, education and community development.

With experience spanning India and New Zealand, her vision is to build partnerships that transform compassion, knowledge and resources into practical action.

Sadhna is an international business professional, social-impact advocate, and practitioner of naturopathy and yoga. Her work is guided by the belief that well-being includes food, clean water, education, hygiene, dignity and opportunities for community self-reliance.

A journey across disciplines

India & New Zealand

Her professional experience spans business management, marketing, international trade, consulting, real estate and product development.

Naturopathy & yoga

Her supplied profile lists a Diploma in Naturopathy and Yoga and a Diploma of Naturopathy with distinction in Hydrotherapy.

Community service

Her experience includes medical and health-awareness camps, natural-health and yoga programs, humanitarian assistance and community education.

LOOKING AHEAD

From individual health
to planetary well-being.

Her vision for the Forum is to connect compassion, knowledge and resources through partnerships that support healthier, stronger and more self-reliant communities.
