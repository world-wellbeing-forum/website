GLOBAL PRESENCE

Across borders.
For a better world.

India and New Zealand form an important part of our international vision: exchanging knowledge, innovation, expertise and humanitarian ideas.

India

A focus on healthcare, medicines, clean water, river restoration, pollution awareness, food support, hygiene, education and community development.

New Zealand

Building connections for future partnerships in health, environmental sustainability, innovation, technology, education and international collaboration.

Building an
international network

Our ambition is to connect people across countries and disciplines to create practical programs for communities.

We welcome conversations with healthcare professionals, researchers, environmental organizations, educators, technology experts, businesses, community leaders and humanitarian organizations.

Partnership development is an ongoing priority; these focus areas do not imply established offices or operating programs in every location.
