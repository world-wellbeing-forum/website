WHAT IS WELLBEING?

A state of balance in body, mind and life.

Wellbeing is a dynamic state of health, balance and contentment in your body, mind, relationships and environment — the ability to cope, grow, connect and thrive in daily life.

Explore the dimensions of wellbeing →

Physical

Movement, nourishment, rest, sleep and preventive care.

Mental

Focus, learning, resilience, clarity and asking for support.

Emotional

Self-awareness, self-compassion and healthy expression.

Social

Family, friendship, belonging and supportive communities.

Spiritual

Meaning, values, reflection, connection and inner peace.

Financial

Knowledge, planning, stability and confidence.

Digital

Intentional technology use, boundaries and time offline.

Professional

Purposeful work, healthy culture and work-life integration.

FOUNDER & CEO

About Dr Sadhna Dixit

Passionate about helping people realise their full potential through work-life integration and wellbeing.

An author, public speaker, alternative medicine advocate and qualified naturopath and yoga teacher, Sadhna has practised yoga for 25 years, learning while living in India and returning regularly to the Himalayas. She is CEO of the World Wellbeing Forum (New Zealand) and Director of Spiritual Earth (Ireland) and Fernleaf Ltd (New Zealand), and has organised the Rejuvenate India events with 500+ participants.
