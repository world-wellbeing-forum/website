OUR SHARED VISION

Well-being for every person.
A healthier future for every community.

The World Well-Being Forum is a platform for holistic wellbeing, education, humanitarian service and environmental responsibility.

We connect individual wellbeing with the conditions that help communities flourish: health knowledge, meaningful relationships, education, opportunity, clean water and a healthy environment.

A WHOLE-PERSON APPROACH

Ten dimensions of well-being

Each dimension influences the others. Lasting wellbeing grows when we care for the whole person and the world around us.

01

Physical health

Movement, nutrition, rest and preventive care.

02

Mental & emotional health

Emotional awareness, resilience and mental wellbeing.

03

Social connection

Relationships, belonging, family and community.

04

Spiritual well-being

Meaning, values, reflection and inner peace.

05

Intellectual growth

Learning, curiosity, creativity and lifelong development.

06

Work-life balance

Purposeful work, healthy boundaries and time to recharge.

07

Environmental health

Clean air, safe water, nature and sustainable choices.

08

Financial security

Financial confidence, stability and informed choices.

09

Community well-being

Safety, inclusion, access and shared opportunity.

10

Purpose & contribution

Using our strengths to contribute to something meaningful.
